Privacy Policy — Lounge Flight Control

Last updated: June 2026

Lounge Flight Control (“the App”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

Lounge Flight Control does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to external servers.

All information entered by users is stored locally on the device, including:

  • Flight information (origin, destination, airline, flight number, dates, and booking reference)
  • Credit card information related to lounge access eligibility
  • Lounge preferences, favorites, and ratings
  • Backup/export files generated manually by the user

2. Local Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device using Android local storage mechanisms. The app does not maintain cloud accounts, remote databases, or user profiles.

3. Data Sharing

Lounge Flight Control does not sell, rent, share, or transfer user data to third parties. Your data remains under your control.

4. External Services

The App may connect to third-party flight information APIs to retrieve flight-related information. These requests are made only to provide app functionality and do not intentionally include personal user data beyond what is necessary.

All external communications are performed over secure HTTPS connections.

5. Backup and Export

The App allows users to export and import backups in JSON format. Backup files are created only when explicitly requested by the user and are handled according to the user’s own actions.

Users are responsible for protecting exported backup files.

6. Data Deletion

Users may delete their data at any time by:

  • Removing flights or cards inside the app
  • Clearing app storage in Android settings
  • Uninstalling the app

7. Children’s Privacy

Lounge Flight Control is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date.
