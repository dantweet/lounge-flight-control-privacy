Privacy Policy - Trip Control

Last updated: October 2026

Trip Control (“the App”) is an Android application designed to help users manage personal travel information, including trips, flights, accommodations, transportation, vehicle rentals, airport lounges, cards, access programs, expenses, travelers, travel guides, notifications, and backups.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, how that information is stored, and when it may be transmitted to external services.

1. Information We Handle

Trip Control is designed primarily around local data storage. Information entered by the user is stored locally on the device unless the user chooses to use a feature that requires communication with an external service.

Depending on the features used, the App may handle:

  • Trip information, including trip names, dates, destinations, and related records;
  • Flight information, including airline, flight number, origin, destination, date, departure and arrival times, seat, and booking reference (PNR);
  • Accommodation information, including property name, dates, reservation information, and amount paid;
  • Transportation information, including transportation type, company, dates, times, confirmation information, and amount paid;
  • Vehicle rental information, including rental company, dates, confirmation information, and amount paid;
  • Traveler information associated with a trip;
  • Travel expense information, including amounts, currencies, participants, and payment information entered by the user;
  • Airport lounge information, including lounge name, airport, location, access programs, amenities, favorites, ratings, and personal notes;
  • Credit card information entered by the user for managing airport lounge benefits, such as card name, issuer, card network, last four digits, benefits, access programs, limits, validity, and usage;
  • Travel guide information, such as places, addresses, telephone numbers, categories, and notes;
  • App settings and preferences;
  • Backup data generated by the App.

2. Credit Card Information

Trip Control does not require users to enter complete credit card numbers, security codes, passwords, or banking credentials.

Card information stored in the App is intended for personal organization and management of airport lounge benefits. Users may enter information such as the card name, issuer, card network, last four digits, access programs, benefits, limits, validity, and usage history.

3. Local Storage

The App stores its primary application data locally on the user's Android device using Android application storage mechanisms and a local database.

Local data may include trips, flights, accommodations, transportation, vehicle rentals, lounges, cards, access programs, benefits, expenses, travelers, travel guides, notifications, settings, and other information entered by the user.

The App does not maintain a general-purpose remote database containing users' personal travel information.

4. Google Account and Google Drive

Trip Control provides optional cloud backup functionality using Google Sign-In and the Google Drive App Data Folder.

When the user chooses to connect a Google account, the App uses Google's authentication mechanisms to authenticate the user. Trip Control does not receive or store the user's Google account password.

When cloud backup is enabled, backup data generated by the App may be uploaded to the private application data area associated with the user's Google Drive account.

The App supports manual backups, automatic backups, restoration, backup history, account switching, and cloud backup management.

Cloud backup is optional. Users can use the App's local functionality without connecting a Google account.

5. Automatic Backups

Trip Control supports automatic cloud backups. When relevant data changes, the App may mark the data as pending for backup and subsequently synchronize it according to the App's backup mechanism.

Automatic backups are not necessarily performed immediately after every individual change. The App uses a controlled synchronization process to avoid unnecessary uploads.

Users who enable cloud backup should understand that information included in the application's backup may be transmitted to and stored in the user's Google Drive App Data Folder.

6. Flight Information Services

Trip Control may use an external flight information service to retrieve flight-related information when the user explicitly requests a flight lookup.

Information required to perform a flight lookup may include flight-related parameters such as airline, flight number, date, origin, or destination.

The flight lookup functionality is used to provide flight information within the App. If the external service is unavailable or does not return a result, the user can continue entering flight information manually.

Communications with external services are performed using secure HTTPS connections.

7. Data Sharing

Trip Control does not sell, rent, or otherwise monetize users' personal information.

The App does not maintain a general-purpose advertising profile based on the user's travel information.

Information may be transmitted to external services only when necessary to provide functionality requested or enabled by the user, including:

  • Google services for authentication and cloud backup;
  • Flight information services for flight lookup functionality.

The App does not intentionally transmit unrelated personal information when performing these operations.

8. Backup and Export

Trip Control supports JSON backup and restoration.

Local backups may contain information stored by the App, depending on the sections included in the backup.

Cloud backups use the same backup data structure used by the App's local backup process.

Users are responsible for protecting exported backup files and for maintaining the security of the Google account used for cloud backups.

9. Data Retention

Locally stored information remains on the device until the user deletes it, clears the application's storage, or uninstalls the App.

Cloud backup data may remain in the user's Google Drive App Data Folder independently of the data stored locally on the device.

Therefore, deleting local App data or uninstalling the App does not necessarily delete previously created cloud backups.

10. Data Deletion

Users can delete local App data by:

  • Deleting individual records within the App;
  • Clearing the App's storage through Android settings;
  • Uninstalling the App.

Cloud backup data is stored in the user's Google Drive App Data Folder. Management or deletion of such cloud data may depend on the cloud backup functionality and the user's Google account.

11. Security

Trip Control uses Android platform storage mechanisms and secure HTTPS communications when communicating with supported external services.

The App does not store Google account passwords.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their Android device, screen lock, Google account, and any exported backup files.

12. Children's Privacy

Trip Control is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

13. Third-Party Services

Trip Control may interact with third-party services required to provide specific functionality, including Google services for authentication and cloud storage and external services used for flight information lookup.

These third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Users should review the privacy policies of the respective services when using those features.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the App, its functionality, or applicable requirements.

Changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date displayed at the beginning of this policy.
